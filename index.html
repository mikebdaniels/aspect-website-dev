The Studio

AspectDesign sits at a rare intersection, full-service agency meets design boutique.

This microsite features a selection of Aspect’s latest work spanning film and television main titles, game cinematics, and featured marketing design and look development.

While the work represents a wide range of styles and capabilities, the thing that ties it all together—and what we pride ourselves on most—is our approach to storytelling. Whatever the project, our goal is to find the right visual language that feels purposeful, distinctive, and genuinely services the story being told.
